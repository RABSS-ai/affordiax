Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect standard anonymous analytics, device metadata, and browser configuration properties (such as screen size, WebGL capabilities for our 3D maps, and general region) to deliver custom responsiveness. When you fill out support forms, we collect your name, email address, and subject context.

2. How We Use Your Data

The inputs you submit into our budget calculators, relocation vectors, and salary analysis forms are computed strictly client-side on your browser and are not recorded permanently or sold to advertising networks. Support form details are used solely to address your technical feedback.

3. Cookies and Analytics

LivingCost USA uses lightweight essential cookies and browser cache variables to remember your default benchmark city selections and localized country filters. Third-party content blocks (such as FontAwesome CDN or Google Web Fonts) may also process standard performance requests.

4. Third-Party Links

Our platform may feature external hypermedia links to maps, real estate databases, or career boards. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external domains.

5. Contact Information

If you have inquiries regarding our data handling or privacy boundaries, please reach out to us through our designated Contact Support portal.

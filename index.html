COMMON INQUIRIES

What people want to know

Research-backed answers to the most frequently searched relocation and cost queries.

How are Crime Indexes determined for cities on LivingCost?

Crime Indexes are compiled using official law enforcement statistics, FBI Uniform Crime Reporting (UCR) databases, and regional safety perception metrics. The rating spans a scale from 0 to 100, where lower numbers represent safer conditions and fewer reported crimes.

How does local crime rate affect overall living costs?

While safety has an indirect relationship with living expenses, high-crime areas often suffer from depressed property values and rent rates. Conversely, safer premium neighborhoods command a pricing premium on housing, higher property taxes, and increased demand for homeowner insurance premiums.

Are there states with exceptionally low crime rates and low taxes?

Yes, states like New Hampshire and Wyoming feature both zero state income tax (or very low tax burden) and consistently rank among the top ten safest states in America, offering a highly cost-efficient and secure lifestyle balance.
